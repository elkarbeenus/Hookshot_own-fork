LaküenRecomp v0.3.1 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S, as v0.3.0, with **shadow quality you can turn down** and a **shut down when done** box for overnight builds.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Shadow quality

- The Graphics tab's **Shadow quality** row goes down instead of up: **Game**, **Medium** (half size), **Low** (a third), **Very low** (a quarter) and **Off** (no shadows drawn). It applies after a restart.
- **Off** also saves the shadow passes' draw calls. On a PC whose CPU feeds the GPU (an RX 6600 XT here), it ran clearly better and quick camera turns in a busy town no longer dragged.
- `LAKUEN_SHADOW_SCALE=<0.25-4>` (any size) and `LAKUEN_SHADOWS=0` (none) in `hookshot_env.txt` decide over the row.

## Shut down when done

- A **Shut down when done** box on the build's and the update's page: when the build ends the device shuts down after a minute's countdown (**Don't shut down** stops it). For a build or an update overnight, so the device is not left on.

## Device profiles (version 2)

The builder offers the updated profiles once more after the update; your current settings are kept first and can be restored under **Device profile**.

| Device | Game profile | Render resolution | Output | Frame generation | Shadows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steam Deck | Handheld | 50 % | – | 2x, paced | Medium |
| Legion Go S | Handheld | 67 % | – | 2x, paced | Medium |
| PC with a graphics card of 8 GB or less | Docked | 75 % | 1920x1080 | 2x, paced | Off |

The PC profile also records the game's draws on 3 threads (`RX_VK_PARALLEL=3` in `hookshot_env.txt`), where the CPU's render thread is the limit.

Everything else is as in [v0.3.0](https://github.com/elkarbeenus/LakuenRecompBuilder/releases/tag/v0.3.0).

## Install

1. Download `LakuenRecomp-v0.3.1-x86_64.AppImage` below, `chmod +x` it and start it.
2. An existing game folder is updated in place; saves stay in `~/.local/share/hookshot`.
