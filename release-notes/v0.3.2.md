LaküenRecomp v0.3.2 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S, as before, with a **lighter handheld profile**: less RAM, fewer effects, and each effect back with one menu row.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## RAM on handhelds

A Steam Deck log showed the system with 339 MB of RAM left when an area loaded, and the frame rate fell to 1-17 fps as it swapped.

- **Shader preparation** (new row on the Graphics tab): **All at start** makes every shader the game folder has seen before the game starts and keeps them in RAM. On the Steam Deck that was 18,713 programs and 25,337 pipelines, about 4 GB. **As needed** makes each one when the game asks for it, from the caches: far less RAM, with an occasional short stutter. It applies after a restart; `RX_VK_PRECOMPILE` in `hookshot_env.txt` decides over it.
- While less than 1.5 GB of RAM is available, the idle upload memory is freed and the heap is trimmed straight away instead of every 30 s.

## Effects

- Four **Effect pass** rows (Graphics tab) turn off, at once, the four costliest passes the Steam Deck's GPU timing found after the shadows. Turn one back on to see what it is; please tell us which ones you miss.
- Use the existing **Upscaler** row's **Game's own** instead of AMD FSR 3.1, which took about 3.6 ms of a Steam Deck's frame.

## Device profiles (version 3, handhelds)

The builder offers the updated profile once more after the update. Your current settings are kept first and can be restored under **Device profile**.

| Device | Render resolution | Upscaler | Shadows | Texture filtering | Effect passes | Shader preparation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steam Deck | 50 % | Game's own | Off | Game's | Off | As needed |
| Legion Go S | 67 % | Game's own | Off | Game's | Off | As needed |

Both keep the game's Handheld profile and 2x paced frame generation. They also record the game's draws on 2 threads (`RX_VK_PARALLEL=2`). The PC profile is unchanged.

Everything else is as in [v0.3.1](https://github.com/elkarbeenus/LakuenRecompBuilder/releases/tag/v0.3.1).
